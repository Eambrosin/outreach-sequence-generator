# Portfolio Demo Walkthrough — ENGAGE

## Goal

Demonstrate how qualification context is converted into a channel-aware, adaptive outreach sequence without leaking internal scores or inventing purchase intent.

## 5-minute walkthrough

### 1. Upload evidence-aware handoff
Upload an ENGAGE Handoff v2 from IDENTIFY.

### 2. Account & Outreach Intelligence Workspace
Show:
- account and contact
- Account Opportunity
- Contact Readiness
- Qualification Readiness
- Sales Motion
- Buyer Access
- territory context

### 3. Upstream Qualification Intelligence
Show:
- commercial hypothesis
- evidence-based commercial angle
- next best action
- evidence gaps
- qualification questions
- validation flags

Key point: these are internal execution inputs, not prospect-facing claims.

### 4. Adaptive strategy
Show:
- priority
- intensity
- language
- primary channel
- cadence

Explain that the primary channel is selected from an observed public path when available.

### 5. Generate sequence
Generate the deterministic or AI-assisted four-touch sequence.

For a research-ready cold account, explain why the cadence is qualification-first rather than aggressive.

### 6. Prospect-facing review
Open each touch and show that:
- internal scores are absent
- buying intent is not assumed
- open questions are framed as questions
- each touch has a different purpose

### 7. Export
Download TXT or CSV.

## One-sentence portfolio pitch

> I built an adaptive outreach engine that preserves upstream commercial evidence, chooses a usable public channel and turns research gaps into qualification-first messaging rather than unsupported sales claims.
